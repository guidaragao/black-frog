Privacy policy · updated September 2026

What we do with what you give us.

Short version: we use it to build and send you your website preview, and to talk to you about it. Nothing else.

What we collect

When you fill a form on blkfrog.com, on Facebook or on Instagram, we get what you typed: your name, email, phone number, business name, and, if you gave it, the address of your current website. If you gave us a website, we read it to prepare the preview.

What we use it for

To build the first version of your site and send it to you, to answer your questions, and to tell you what happens next. If you say no, we stop. Nobody follows up after a no.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone outside Black Frog except the services we need to run (email delivery, hosting, and Meta, where the form lives). We do not add you to a newsletter.

How long we keep it

As long as we are talking, and up to 12 months after the last contact, unless you become a client. Ask and we delete it.

Cookies

This site keeps your cookie choice in your browser, so we do not ask twice. Our fonts come from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address to deliver them, as on most websites.

If you press Accept, we also load the Meta Pixel. It uses cookies to tell us, and Meta, when someone visits the site or sends a form, so we can measure our ads and show them to people they fit. If you choose Essential only, the Pixel never loads.

Your choices

Want to see what we have, fix it, or delete it? Write to hello@blkfrog.com and we do it within a few days.
